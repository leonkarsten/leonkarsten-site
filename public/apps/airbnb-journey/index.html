    How to read it

    Each row is one chain: what happened on the left (brown for a human need, gold for a business opportunity), what Airbnb did about it in the middle, tagged by the kind of innovation it was, and what came of it on the right, with a measured result where I could find one, and an honest blank where I couldn't. Releases that simply came off the roadmap are folded into a single line.

    Two things stand out. The radical moves are rare and early: the idea itself, and Experiences nine years later. Almost everything since is incremental, which is what a company that has found its model is supposed to do. And the launches people remember most warmly, housing displaced residents after Sandy, Frontline Stays, Ukraine housing, all came from responding to a need rather than from a plan.
